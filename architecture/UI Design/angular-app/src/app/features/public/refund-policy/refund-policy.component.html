1. Cancellation Window

Bookings can be cancelled free of charge within the applicable cancellation window (see table below). Cancellations made after this window may incur a cancellation fee to compensate the assigned transporter.

Booking TypeFree CancellationFee After Window
Full Truck LoadUp to 2 hours before pickup10% of booking value
Part LoadUp to 4 hours before pickup15% of booking value
Parcel DeliveryUp to 1 hour before pickup₹50 flat fee

2. Refund Process

Eligible refunds are initiated within 24 hours of cancellation and credited back to your original payment method within 5-7 business days, depending on your bank or payment provider.

3. Non-Refundable Charges

  • Detention or waiting charges incurred due to delays at pickup/drop location.
  • Toll, parking or additional charges already paid on your behalf.
  • Bookings cancelled after the vehicle has departed for pickup.

4. Payment Disputes

If you notice an incorrect charge, please raise a support ticket within 7 days of the transaction. Our finance team will investigate and resolve valid disputes within 10 business days.

5. How to Request a Refund

You can request a refund directly from your Bookings dashboard, or by contacting our support team at support@transportseva.com with your booking ID.
