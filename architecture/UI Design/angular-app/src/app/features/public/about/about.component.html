Our Story

Built by Logistics People, for Logistics People

TransportSeva was founded in 2019 with a simple mission: make road freight in India as easy, transparent and reliable as booking a cab. What started as a small team solving truck-booking headaches for local businesses in Noida has grown into a pan-India platform trusted by thousands of shippers and transporters.

Today, we power millions of kilometres of freight movement every month across 28 states, with live GPS tracking, digital proof-of-delivery, instant payments and a 24x7 support desk that never sleeps.

Our vision is simple — a future where every truck on Indian roads runs full, every shipper gets real-time visibility, and every driver gets paid fairly and on time.
