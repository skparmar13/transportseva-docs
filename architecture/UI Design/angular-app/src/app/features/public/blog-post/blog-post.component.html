How AI is Transforming Freight Route Optimization in India

Home/Blog/Article
12 Jan 2026Neha Mehta6 min read

India's road freight industry moves over 4.5 billion tonnes of goods every year, yet nearly 40% of truck capacity still runs empty on return trips. At TransportSeva, we've spent the last two years building AI-driven route optimization models that tackle this exact problem — matching empty-leg capacity with new bookings in real time.

The Problem With Traditional Route Planning

Most fleet operators still plan routes manually using experience and phone calls. This works at small scale, but breaks down quickly once a fleet crosses 50+ vehicles across multiple states. Fuel costs balloon, drivers wait idle for return loads, and shippers face unpredictable pricing.

How Our Model Works

Our optimization engine ingests live GPS data, historical lane demand, toll costs and driver duty-hour limits to recommend the most efficient route and the best-matched return load within seconds of a booking request.

  • Reduces empty-running distance by up to 22% on major corridors.
  • Cuts average fuel spend per trip by 9-14%.
  • Improves on-time delivery rates through dynamic ETA recalculation.
"The AI routing engine has completely changed how we plan our fleet's week. We're running fuller trucks and spending less time on the phone chasing return loads." — Fleet Owner, Ludhiana

What's Next

We're now extending the model to factor in weather disruptions and mandi/market seasonality for agricultural freight, with a wider rollout planned across all 28 states TransportSeva operates in through 2026.
