1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us, such as when you create an account, book a shipment, or contact our support team. This includes your name, email address, phone number, company details, GST information and payment details.

  • Account information: name, email, mobile number, password (encrypted).
  • Shipment details: pickup/drop addresses, goods description, vehicle preferences.
  • Location data: live GPS location during active bookings for tracking purposes.
  • Device information: IP address, browser type, and app usage analytics.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to provide, maintain and improve our services, including to process bookings, facilitate payments, provide live tracking, send important notifications, and offer customer support.

  • To match shippers with verified transporters and drivers.
  • To process payments and generate GST-compliant invoices.
  • To send booking confirmations, tracking updates and support responses.
  • To improve our AI-based route optimization and pricing models.

3. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share limited information with transporters/drivers assigned to your booking (name, phone, addresses) solely to fulfil the shipment, and with payment processors to complete transactions securely.

4. Data Security

We use industry-standard encryption, secure servers and access controls to protect your data. All payment transactions are processed through PCI-DSS compliant partners. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

5. Your Rights & Choices

You may access, update or request deletion of your personal information at any time from your account settings, or by contacting our support team at privacy@transportseva.com.

6. Cookies & Tracking

We use cookies and similar technologies to remember your preferences, keep you signed in, and understand how you use our platform so we can improve it. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at privacy@transportseva.com or write to us at 123 Logistics Park, Sector 62, Noida, Uttar Pradesh, 201309.
